Foundational Principles

Definitions & Core Framework

🧠

Al-'Aql العَقْل

Reason & Intellect

  • Faculty of Discernment: God-given capability to think, reflect, synthesize logic, and observe creation.
  • Scope: Understands empirical observation, science, logic, and deduces the application of Shariah.
  • Role in Islam: Prerequisites for moral responsibility (Takleef).
📖

An-Naql النَّقْل

Divine Revelation

  • Transmitted Truth: Divine knowledge communicated by Allah (SWT) through Scriptures (Quran) and Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Sunnah).
  • Scope: Unseen realm (Ghaib), ultimate purpose, objective ethics, and divine legislation.
  • Role in Islam: Infallible guidance that anchors human reason.

Golden Rule of Harmony (Dar' Ta'arud)

"Authentic revelation (An-Naql As-Sahih) will never contradict sound explicit reason (Al-'Aql As-Sarih). If a perceived contradiction arises, either the revelation is unauthentic or the logic is flawed."

— Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah (Dar' Ta'arud al-Aql wa al-Naql)

The Perfect Harmony

Synthesis & Conclusion

The Eye & Light Analogy

Imam Al-Ghazali explained: 'Aql (Intellect) is like the Human Eye, and Naql (Revelation) is like Sun Light.

• A healthy eye in pitch darkness cannot see anything.

• Sunlight without an eye cannot produce vision.

• True guidance occurs ONLY when the Eye ('Aql) receives Light (Naql)!

Islamic Golden Age Impact

Muslim polymaths (Ibn Al-Haytham, Al-Biruni, Ibn Sina) were direct products of combining Naql (Quranic duty to study creation) with 'Aql (empirical scientific method).

Key Insight for Y12 Project:

Islam never separated secular science from sacred theology; empirical discovery was viewed as an act of worship (Ibadah).

Final Summary Takeaway

Reason ('Aql) is our instrument to understand divine revelation, while Revelation (Naql) is our absolute compass that safeguards reason from moral corruption and intellectual arrogance. Together, they guide humanity toward truth in this life and success in the Hereafter.
